### About

I build and optimize inference systems for large language models. Currently a core contributor to [SGLang-Omni](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang-omni), where I lead TTS inference optimization — delivering **+103% throughput** and **-51% RTF** on H200 through CUDA Graph migration, async GPU-CPU decode, and LRU encoder caching.

I'm a Member of Technical Staff at RadixArk, working on SGLang community dev-rel and blog writing. Previously a software engineer at Salesforce.

**B.A. Computer Science & Mathematics, University of Virginia** — graduated with High Distinction.
